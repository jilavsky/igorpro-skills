## <strong>beta</strong> (<em>a</em>, <em>b</em>) {#beta}

</div>

The beta function is defined for real or complex arguments as

$$
\displaystyle B(a, b)=\frac{\Gamma(a) \Gamma(b)}{\Gamma(a+b)},
$$

with Re(a), Re(b)>0. Γ is the gamma function.
