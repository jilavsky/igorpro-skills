## <strong>StatsInvRectangularCDF</strong> (<em>cdf</em>, <em>a</em>, <em>b</em>) {#statsinvrectangularcdf}

</div>

The StatsInvRectangularCDF function returns the inverse of the rectangular (uniform) cumulative distribution function

$$
\displaystyle x=a+ {cdf}(b-a) .
$$

where *a* < *b*.
