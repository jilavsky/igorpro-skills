## <strong>sinh</strong> (<em>num</em>) {#sinh}

</div>

The sinh function returns the hyperbolic sine of *num* :

$$
\displaystyle \sinh (x)=\frac{e^{x}-e^{-x}}{2}.
$$

In complex expressions, *num* is complex, and sinh(*num*) returns a complex value.
