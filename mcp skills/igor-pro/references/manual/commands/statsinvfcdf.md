## <strong>StatsInvFCDF</strong> (<em>x</em>, <em>n1</em>, <em>n2</em>) {#statsinvfcdf}

</div>

The StatsInvFCDF function returns the inverse of the F distribution CDF for *x* and shape parameters *n1* and *n2*. The inverse is also known as the percent point function.
