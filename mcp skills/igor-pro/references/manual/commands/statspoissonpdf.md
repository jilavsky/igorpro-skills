## <strong>StatsPoissonPDF</strong> (<em>x</em>, λ) {#statspoissonpdf}

</div>

The StatsPoissonPDF function returns the Poisson probability distribution function.

$$
\displaystyle f(x ; \lambda)=\frac{\exp (-\lambda) \lambda^{x}}{x!}, \quad x=0,1,2 \ldots
$$

where λ is the shape parameter.
