## <strong>cosh</strong> (<em>num</em>) {#cosh}

</div>

The cosh function returns the hyperbolic cosine of *num* :

$$
\displaystyle \cosh (x)=\frac{e^{x}+e^{-x}}{2}.
$$

In complex expressions, *num* is complex, and cosh(*num*) returns a complex value.
