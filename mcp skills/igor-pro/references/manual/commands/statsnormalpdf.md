## <strong>StatsNormalPDF</strong> (<em>x</em>, µ, σ) {#statsnormalpdf}

</div>

The StatsNormalPDF function returns the normal probability distribution function.

$$
\displaystyle f(x, \mu, \sigma)=\frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2 \pi}} \exp \left(-\frac{(x-\mu)^{2}}{2 \sigma^{2}}\right) .
$$
