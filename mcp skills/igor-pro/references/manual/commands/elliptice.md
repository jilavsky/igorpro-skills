## <strong>EllipticE</strong> (<em>x</em>) {#elliptice}

</div>

The EllipticE function returns the complete elliptic integral of the second kind,

$$
\displaystyle E(k)=\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\sqrt{1-k^{2} t^{2}} d t}{\sqrt{1-t^{2}}}
$$

with

$$
\displaystyle |k| \leq 1 .
$$
