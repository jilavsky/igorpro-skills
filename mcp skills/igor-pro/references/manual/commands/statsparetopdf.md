## <strong>StatsParetoPDF</strong> (<em>x</em>, <em>a</em>, <em>c</em>) {#statsparetopdf}

</div>

The StatsParetoPDF function returns the Pareto probability distribution function.

$$
\displaystyle \mathrm{f}(\mathrm{x} ; \mathrm{a}, \mathrm{c})=\frac{\mathrm{c}}{\mathrm{x}}\left(\frac{a}{x}\right)^{c}, \quad  \begin{array}{c} a, c>0 \\ x \ge a \end{array}.
$$
