## <strong>EllipticK</strong> (<em>x</em>) {#elliptick}

</div>

The EllipticK function returns the complete elliptic integral of the first kind,

$$
\displaystyle K(k)=\int_{0}^{1} \frac{d t}{\sqrt{\left(1-t^{2}\right)\left(1-k^{2} t^{2}\right)}},
$$

with

$$
\displaystyle 0 \leq k \leq 1 .
$$
